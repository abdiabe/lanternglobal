## Prepare one place for the work

List priorities, instructions, points of contact, and your working hours in one shared location. Keep the first week focused on a small number of representative tasks rather than every possible responsibility.

## Work through an example together

Show one completed task and explain why it is correct. Have the professional walk through a similar example, then discuss any unclear instructions. Agree how questions should be raised and when the next review will happen.

## Make boundaries explicit

State who approves expenses, customer exceptions, and changes to business systems. Grant only the access needed for the agreed work. Confirm confidentiality expectations and the process for returning or removing access when responsibilities change.
