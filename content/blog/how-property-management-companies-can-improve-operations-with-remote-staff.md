## Separate coordination from decisions

Appointment reminders, maintenance request tracking, and document organization can be described as distinct administrative tasks. Your authorized staff retain decisions about leases, spending, tenant eligibility, and disputes.

## Describe a complete request

Give each request an owner, a status, and a next action. Explain when to contact a vendor, which messages need approval, and how urgent issues should be escalated. Use examples with personal details removed when discussing the role.

## Agree access before work starts

Identify which records the role needs and who grants access. Define a communication schedule and review the process with your team. Start your inquiry with the tasks and tools, not tenant records.
