## Our current approach

At launch, the Lantern Global website does not use advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or third-party analytics. Because no nonessential tracking is active, we do not display a cookie consent banner.

## Cookies and storage that may be used

| Technology | Purpose | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosting and security cookies set by our hosting provider | Deliver pages reliably and protect against abuse | Strictly necessary |
| Cloudflare Turnstile (only if spam protection is enabled on forms) | Distinguish people from automated bots when a form is submitted | Strictly necessary |
| Scheduling provider (only if you open an external scheduling link) | Operate the booking page on the provider's own site | Governed by the provider's policy |

Strictly necessary technologies do not require consent under most privacy laws because the website cannot function securely without them.

## If we add analytics or marketing tools

Before adding analytics, advertising, or other nonessential tracking, we will:

1. Update this policy to list each tool and its purpose
2. Add a consent interface that blocks nonessential tools until you opt in where required
3. Give you a way to change your choice at any time

## Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the website works.
